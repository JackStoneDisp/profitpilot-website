Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Information we collect

When you use our contact form we collect the details you provide: name, email, phone (optional), MC/DOT number, equipment type, number of trucks and your message. Form submissions are processed by Formspree, a third-party form service, and delivered to our email.

We use your information only to reply to you and to provide dispatching and back-office services. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

Our homepage loads live oil price widgets from TradingView, a third-party service that may set its own cookies. We may also use privacy-friendly analytics to understand site traffic. If that changes, we will update this page.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure.
