Carrier Onboarding

Ready to start? We keep onboarding simple and fast. Have your active MC/DOT authority, W-9, and Certificate of Insurance (COI) ready.

How to get started:

  • Send your documents: Email your setup packet to dispatch@profitpilotlogistics.com.
  • Quick verification: We will review your authority and insurance to ensure everything is active and compliant.
  • Get dispatched: Once verified, we will discuss your preferred lanes, equipment, and minimum rate requirements. Then, we get to work.
