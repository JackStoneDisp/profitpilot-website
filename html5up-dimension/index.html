Welcome to ProfitPilot

At ProfitPilot Logistics, we specialize in empowering independent owner-operators and fleet owners across North America. We handle the heavy lifting behind the scenes so you can focus on driving and maximizing your rate-per-mile.

From aggressive rate negotiations with top brokers to handling paperwork, rate confirmations, and billing support, we operate as your dedicated back-office partner.

Our Services

What We Do:

  • Top-Rate Negotiation: Direct broker communication to secure top-paying loads.
  • Dedicated Lane Search: Consistent route planning for Dry Van, Reefer, Flatbed, and Step Deck.
  • Back-Office & Paperwork: Carrier setup packets, NOA, COI updates, and rate confirmation management.
  • Invoicing & Factoring Support: Fast paperwork submission so you get paid without delay.
  • Zero Forced Dispatch: You maintain 100% control over where and when you drive.

Why Choose Us

Your Load. Your Profit. Our Priority.

We pride ourselves on full transparency, clear communication, and dedicated support. Unlike large call-center dispatchers, we provide personalized attention to every carrier we partner with.
