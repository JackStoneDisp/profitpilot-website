Frequently asked questions

Do you force dispatch?

Never. You keep 100% control over your routes and loads.

How much does dispatch cost?

A flat 5% commission per load, with no upfront costs, no hidden administration fees and no long-term contracts.

What do I need to get started?

An active MC/DOT authority, a W-9 and a Certificate of Insurance (COI). Email them to dispatch@profitpilotlogistics.com.

What equipment do you dispatch?

Dry van, reefer, flatbed and step deck, power only and box trucks.

What do you handle besides finding loads?

Rate negotiation with brokers, carrier setup packets, NOA and COI updates, rate confirmations, and invoicing and factoring paperwork.
