How the estimate works

Costs are estimates per mile (fuel and DEF about $0.65, maintenance and tires $0.20, fixed overhead $0.32, tolls and misc $0.08, meals and per diem $0.07) plus a percentage of gross pay (5% dispatch, 2% factoring if you use it). Turn any line off to match your own costs. Results are estimates, not guarantees. See typical fuel costs on the freight hub diesel page.
